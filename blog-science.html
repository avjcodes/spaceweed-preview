The Science Behind Flash Frozen Cured™: A Leap Forward in Cannabis Post-Harvest Processing

Cannabis growing has seen big advances in genetics, lighting and environmental controls, but post-harvest processing has barely changed. Traditional drying and curing often means hanging plants in climate-controlled rooms for 10 to 21 days. That takes a lot of time and labor, and results can vary from batch to batch.

Flash Frozen Curing™ is a patented process that uses cryogenic freezing and lyophilization (freeze-drying) to create a new category of cannabis and make post-harvest work more efficient.

What is Flash Frozen Curing?

It's a two-step post-harvest process:

  • Flash freezing. Fresh cannabis flower is frozen at ultra-low temperatures, typically below -40°C, within an hour of harvest. This stops the plant's enzyme activity.
  • Lyophilization (freeze-drying). The frozen flower goes into a vacuum chamber, where the ice turns straight to vapor without melting first. That's sublimation. This step keeps the flower's cellular structure intact.

Why it works: the science

Traditional hang-drying relies on water evaporating from the flower in warm, humid conditions. That can lead to:

  • Uneven drying, with some buds over-dried and others still holding pockets of moisture.
  • Long exposure to oxygen and light while the flower dries.

Flash Frozen Curing works differently:

  • Deep freezing stops enzyme activity in the plant.
  • Sublimation under vacuum removes the water without applying heat.
  • Oxygen exposure is kept to a minimum while the flower dries.

The result: a new category of cannabis

Flash Frozen Cured™ flower is different from hang-dried flower:

  • Freeze-dried flower keeps its structure, so at the same weight it often looks 1.5 to 2 times the size of hang-dried flower.
  • Automated processes give repeatable results, batch after batch.

Industry impact

For growers and processors, Flash Frozen Curing offers:

  • A product that stands out in a crowded market.

For consumers, the flower keeps more of how it looked on the plant. The buds keep their size, shape and color.

How Flash Frozen Cured works

Flash Frozen Curing is a patented process. Fresh cannabis is frozen, then dried under vacuum using temperature, time and pressure. No solvents or chemicals are ever used.

See the products
Vacuum chamber

The process

Three steps

T+0 · At harvest

01Harvest

The plant is harvested at peak ripeness.

T+ <60 min · Frozen

02Freeze

The flower is frozen within an hour of harvest.

Vacuum · Sublimation

03Dry under vacuum

In the vacuum chamber the ice turns straight to vapor without melting first. That's sublimation, and it takes out over 80% of the water weight.

In the jar

What that means in the jar

  • 01Buds keep their size, shape and color.
  • 02It breaks up by hand, no grinder needed.
  • 03A gram fills the jar like two.
